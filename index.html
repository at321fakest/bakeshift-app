BAKESHIFT

Your recipes, shopping list and meal plan, saved in your own Google Drive. Free, and nobody else can see your recipes.

Sign in with Google

Google will ask you to approve Bakeshift:

  1. Pick your Google account.
  2. On Google hasn't verified this app, tap Advanced, then Go to Bakeshift.
  3. Tick Select all, then tap Continue.
Sign in with Google

Done. Your Bakeshift folder is ready in Google Drive.

Sign in again

2

Add it to your home screen

In Chrome, tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen, then Install.

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

The first time you open it from the home screen, sign in to Google once more. iPhones keep home-screen apps signed in separately.

On a computer, use Chrome or Edge: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar. Or just bookmark this page.

Installed. Open Bakeshift from your home screen.

Open Bakeshift in this browser
